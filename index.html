1. Introduction

Sulfuric acid (H₂SO₄) is one of the most widely produced industrial chemicals in the world. It plays a crucial role in fertilizer production, petroleum refining, metal processing, chemical synthesis, and wastewater treatment.

This project presents the complete engineering design of a 1000 ton per day sulfuric acid plant using the Contact Process with 98% efficiency. The study includes detailed process explanation, stoichiometric calculations, equipment selection, capital cost estimation, operational cost analysis, and full economic feasibility evaluation.

2. Overall Process Description

The plant operates using the modern Contact Process. Sulfur is combusted to produce sulfur dioxide (SO₂). The gas is cleaned and dried before entering a catalytic converter, where SO₂ is oxidized to sulfur trioxide (SO₃). The SO₃ is absorbed in concentrated sulfuric acid forming oleum, which is subsequently diluted to produce commercial sulfuric acid.

  • Stage 1 – Sulfur Burning
  • Stage 2 – Gas Cleaning & Drying
  • Stage 3 – Catalytic Conversion
  • Stage 4 – Absorption (Oleum Formation)
  • Stage 5 – Dilution
