1. Introduction

Sulfuric acid (H₂SO₄) is one of the most important industrial chemicals worldwide. It is widely used in fertilizer production, petroleum refining, mineral processing, chemical manufacturing, and wastewater treatment. The production capacity of sulfuric acid is often considered an indicator of industrial development.

This project presents the detailed engineering design of a 1000 ton per day sulfuric acid plant using the Contact Process with 98% efficiency. The study covers process description, stoichiometric calculations, equipment selection, capital cost estimation, operational cost evaluation, and economic feasibility analysis.

2. Overall Process Description

The production of sulfuric acid in this plant follows the modern double absorption contact process. The process consists of five major stages:

  • Sulfur Burning (SO₂ generation)
  • Gas Cleaning and Drying
  • Catalytic Oxidation of SO₂ to SO₃
  • Absorption of SO₃ to form Oleum
  • Dilution to obtain commercial H₂SO₄

Each stage is designed to maximize conversion efficiency while maintaining safe and stable operating conditions.

3. Unit 1 – Sulfur Burning Furnace (F-101)

3.1 Process Description

Molten sulfur is combusted in dry air inside a refractory-lined furnace. The combustion produces sulfur dioxide (SO₂) gas in a highly exothermic reaction.

S + O₂ → SO₂
